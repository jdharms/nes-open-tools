* Adds a per-seed "Yardage Book" for every seed.  This is essentially the site's Rangefinder,
but unique for the 18 holes comprising the specific seed.  It renders the *exact* data
written into the rom, including any potential edits or transformations the randomization
process applies.  It also displays the pin position for the seed, as well as the exact
wind direction and strength for the tee shot.
