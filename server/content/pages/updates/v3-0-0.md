* Players can set BGM, Swing Speed, Putt Speed, and Swing Spin defaults at seed download time.
* Players' choices for these settings are remembered when downloading a new seed.
* The settings in the download panel are now collapsed by default.
* Players' /me pages show their remembered choices and allow edits.
* Newly generated seeds with club restrictions remove **CHOOSE CLUBS** from the in-game clubhouse menu.
* Newly generated seeds record fairways hit and total water/out-of-bounds penalty strokes.
