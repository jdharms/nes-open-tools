* The Wind Fix.  In the vanilla game, eight of the 16 directions were wrong due
to reading opcodes as data.  Most visibly, straight crosswinds also pushed the ball
either up (West crosswind) or down (East crosswind).  New randomizer seeds include
a fix, so wind that is "due west" acts the same as wind that is "due north", just shifted
90 degrees.
* Adds ability to limit expert holes.  Expert holes are defined as Mario Open holes that
play tougher against par than any NES Open hole.  The default is one expert hole per nine.
* Wind profiles.  Two new settings:
  * Speed profile: this setting shapes the average speed of the wind over the course of the round.
  * Directon profile: this setting shapes the average direction of the wind over the course of the round.
