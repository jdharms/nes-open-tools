## Who we are

This site hosts a randomizer and a collection of tools and information related to
the games **NES Open Tournament Golf** and **Mario Open Golf**.  This site is
fan-run, and has no affiliation with Nintendo or any of its partners.

For any questions regarding privacy or user data, please contact
`privacy [at sign] nesopengolf [dot] com`. (Replace the [at sign] with @, [dot] with . and remove all spaces.)

## What we collect

Users can sign in to the site using Discord OAuth.  We only request the `identify`
scope, which consists of access to user's Discord user ID, username, display name,
and avatar.  Discord provides OAuth "access" and "refresh" tokens which we use
to keep users signed in.

On this site, users can generate data, broadly categorized as game scores and
game activity.  Game data includes, but isn't limited to rounds played,
round/hole scores, and activity timestamps.

On the technical side, IP addresses may be written to server logs, and session
cookies are used for login.

## How we use it

Discord identity data is used for authenticating users, facilitating scored
games, and displaying leaderboards.  Usernames and scores are visible to
other users, including signed-out visitors.

Technical data such as IP addresses and any other information written to
server logs is only used for diagnosing bugs and tracking overall server
load.  Logs are rotated on a regular basis and no attempt is made at
tracking individual users' activity.

## Who we share data with

We sell no data.  Service providers process it on our behalf: Linode hosts
the server and Backblaze stores encrypted database backups. Currently
all data is stored in the United States.

## Cookies and local storage

A session cookie is used to keep users signed in.  Local storage is used
to verify that users have the **NES Open Tournament Golf** and/or
**Mario Open Golf** ROM files and to apply patches for users.  The local
storage can be cleared in the ROM Setup page, linked in this site's header.

ROM files remain in your browser and are *never* uploaded to our server.

A cookie is used to save download settings (player name, club bag, etc.)
as a convenience to the player.  It contains only the data entered by
the player in the Download panel, with no Discord or internal ID values.
The free-text "Player Name" field is included in this cookie.

## Retention and deletion

Information is kept as long as users have an active account with the site.
Users may request deletion by emailing the privacy address in the "Who we are"
section above.  Users may revoke our access to their Discord identity details
at any time from their Discord authorized apps settings.  Potentially identifying
technical details on the server (IP Addresses, etc.) are anonymized or removed
within 90 days.

When users request deletion of account data, their scores will be
removed from leaderboards, but scoring data may be kept in the database
but tied to an anonymized User ID instead of anything personal or identifiable.

## Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 in most regions.  This site
is not aimed at children under 13.

## Your rights

You can request a copy, correction, or deletion of your data at the privacy address
above. We process it to provide the site you signed up for (contract) and to keep
it running securely (legitimate interests). Users in the EU or UK may also file a
complaint with their local data protection authority.

## Changes

We will handle changes to this policy based on what changes:

* Contact emails: updated in this page along with a summary of changes in the Version information section
* What we collect: updated in this page. If additional Discord information is requested, users will be notified by Discord on their next sign-in.
* How we use it, who we share with, retention periods, analytics, etc: A notice will be provided to each user the next time they open the site, as we keep no contact information.  Users will either be able to opt-out or enough notice will be given for removal requests/feedback to occur, **at least 30 days**.

## Do Not Track

This site performs no cross-site tracking and treats all visitors the same
regardless of Do Not Track signals.
