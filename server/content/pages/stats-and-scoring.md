This site keeps track of your stats and scores for the rounds you play!

## How a score gets posted

If you are signed-in via Discord when downloading a seed, two things are
inserted into the ROM when patching it: your player ID and a secret key
that is unique to you for that seed.  After you complete the round and
dismiss the scorecard, a QR code is generated by the game and displayed.

When you scan this QR code with a phone or tablet, the site records your
stats for the round.  You don't need to be signed in on the device
that scans the code, because the secret key inserted into the ROM
is used to authenticate the submission.

After the stats are recorded, your browser will be redirected to the
"Round page" for the submission.  This is a permanent, publicly-accessible
page you can use to see your stats for the round.  You can find all your
completed round pages on your [/me](/me) page.

## What's recorded

The vanilla game keeps track of your strokes and putts for each hole.
In addition to this, the randomizer currently also tracks:

* Fairways hit.  For each par 4 and par 5, if your tee shot ends up on the fairway or green, you get credit for hitting that fairway.  Par 3s are ignored.
* Penalty strokes. The total number of strokes added for water or out of bounds.
* A timestamp of when it was recorded.

## Guest ROMs

If you're not signed-in when downloading the ROM, you can still play the game.
However, since we'd have no way to know who to assign the stats to, the game
doesn't generate a QR code after the final scorecard.  We refer to these as
"Guest ROMs".

There's no way to convert a Guest ROM to a player-assigned ROM.  You'll need
to sign-in on the site and save the patched ROM again.

## One round per player per seed

The first round you submit for a seed is the one that counts.  Scanning it
again, or scanning a later round on the same seed, won't add any new stats,
and will redirect you to the original submitted round.

## Teammate rounds (Player 2)

Currently, if you play a 2-player game using a randomizer ROM, both players
will get a QR code to scan after the round.  There's not yet a way to assign
the 2nd player to another user, so scanning it will mark it as "Player 1's
Teammate".

## Where rounds are displayed

Rounds, in either complete or summary form, appear in a few different places:

* The seed page's Completed Rounds table.  This lets multiple players compete
and compare scores for the seed.
* Your [/me](/me) page.  This lets you see all your completed rounds.
* The round's page itself.  This displays all the data the round contains.
